Support — Scope Change Orders

How to reach us

Email team@finalmark.io.

Please include your Atlassian site URL, the title of the change request, and what you expected to happen. A screenshot of the Jira issue panel helps a lot.

Response times

Scope Change Orders is in free beta. We aim to reply within 2 business days (Monday to Friday, Swiss time). Security reports are handled first: critical issues are fixed within the timeframes of Atlassian's Security Bug Fix Policy.

Supported products

  • Jira Cloud (Software, Work Management, Service Management).
  • Confluence is not supported yet.

Common questions

"Only administrators of project X can approve" — but I lead the project. Internal approval is limited to project administrators by default. A Jira administrator can open it to any user in Jira settings → Apps → Scope Change Settings.

The client link does not work, or the client sees "already approved". Links expire after 7 days, and a link stops accepting decisions once a decision is recorded. Generate a new link from the issue panel; the earlier decision stays in the audit trail.

No comment appears on the Jira issue. Either audit comments are switched off in Scope Change Settings, or the person taking the action is not allowed to comment on that issue. The workflow step itself still succeeds.

Times in the exported document do not match Jira. Documents show times in the timezone of the person who created the request, and always name that timezone, so a client reads the same time wherever they are.

Can I delete a change request? Not yet. Requests form an audit trail on purpose. Tell us if you need archiving.
