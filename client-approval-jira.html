How to get client approval in Jira without an Atlassian account

Your team works in Jira. Your client does not. Here are the options for getting a recorded client decision on a Jira issue without giving the client a Jira licence.

The options

OptionGood forLimits
Add the client as a Jira userLong projects with a very involved clientCosts a licence seat; the client sees more than you may want
Jira Service Management portalSupport requests from customersBuilt for tickets, not for commercial sign-off
Email the client, attach the replyOccasional approvalsNo structure; easy to lose; nothing links cost to the decision
A secure approval link from an appChange orders and paid changesNeeds a Marketplace app

How the approval link works in Scope Change Orders

  1. Your team creates a change request from the Jira issue and a project admin approves it internally.
  2. You copy a client link and send it by your usual channel.
  3. The client opens a clean page outside Jira showing the change, the price and the timeline impact.
  4. They approve or reject and type their name. A rejection asks for a reason.
  5. The decision is recorded on the Jira issue, and the link stops accepting decisions.

Links expire after 7 days. Internal notes never appear on the client page or in the exported document. The app runs on Atlassian Forge and stores everything on your own Atlassian site.
