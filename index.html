Turn out-of-scope work into approved change orders, inside Jira

A client asks for "one more variant". The team delivers it. Nobody bills it. Scope Change Orders turns that moment into a priced, approved and client-signed change order, without leaving the Jira issue where the request appeared.

How it works

  1. Capture it where it happens. Create a change request from any Jira issue. Project, work item and requester are prefilled; you add the budget and timeline impact.
  2. Approve internally. Project admins approve or reject. A rejection needs a reason, and every step lands in an audit trail.
  3. Let the client sign. Send a secure approval link. The client approves or rejects without an Atlassian account.
  4. Export and invoice. Download a client-ready change order with the full approval trail. Internal notes never leave your team.

Built for agencies and delivery teams

Most Jira approval apps route internal sign-offs. Scope Change Orders is about money: the work your team did beyond the agreed scope, and whether the client agreed to pay for it. A dashboard shows recovered, pending and absorbed revenue, so you can see how much scope drift costs you.

Your data stays on your Atlassian site

The app runs on Atlassian Forge. Everything is stored in Atlassian-hosted storage for your site, and the app makes no calls outside Atlassian. Read the privacy policy.
