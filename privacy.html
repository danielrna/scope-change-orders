Privacy Policy — Scope Change Orders

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Scope Change Orders ("the App") is a Jira Cloud app built on Atlassian Forge and published by FinalMark, operated by Daniel Roina, Switzerland ("we"). Contact: team@finalmark.io.

What the App stores

The App stores only what your users enter or what the workflow records:

  • change request content: title, summary, reason, client or account name, budget and timeline impact, affected Jira work items;
  • approval records: the internal approver's name and optional email, the name typed by the client approver, decision notes and timestamps;
  • the event history of each change request, including the display name of the Jira user who performed each step and the timezone of the user who created the request;
  • workspace settings: branding, currency, default approver, approval policy and whether audit comments are posted;
  • temporary access tokens for client approval and export links, which expire after 7 days.

Some of these fields are personal data (names, emails). They are entered by your users for your own business process.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored in Atlassian Forge hosted storage attached to your Atlassian site. The App has no servers, databases or third-party services of its own, and makes no network calls outside Atlassian. We never receive a copy of your content.

What the App reads from Atlassian

  • The Jira issue a request is created from (summary, project, assignee), read as the current user, to prefill the request.
  • The current user's display name and timezone, to record who did what and when.
  • The current user's Jira permissions, to decide who may approve internally and whether an audit comment may be posted. These results are not stored.

What the App writes to Jira

If enabled in Scope Change Settings, the App adds a comment to the originating Jira issue at each workflow step. Internal decision notes are never included in these comments. Jira then notifies watchers according to your own notification scheme.

Links shared with clients

Client approval and export links are served by Atlassian Forge web triggers. Anyone holding a link can open it until it expires, so share it only with the intended recipient. Internal approval notes never appear on client-facing pages or documents.

Retention and deletion

Data stays in Forge storage while the App is installed. When the App is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes it according to the Forge hosted storage data lifecycle.

Once a week, the App reports the Atlassian account IDs it holds to Atlassian's personal data reporting API, as Atlassian requires of every app that stores personal data:

  • when Atlassian reports an account as closed, the App replaces that person's name and account ID with "Former user" everywhere and deletes their timezone;
  • when Atlassian reports that an account's details changed, the App refreshes the stored name and timezone.

Client-facing documents are generated on demand and are not stored.

Your rights

We do not hold your data, so requests about the content of change requests are handled by the Jira administrators of your site. For any question about this policy, write to team@finalmark.io.

Changes

We will publish any change to this policy on this page, with a new date.
